## Why AGPL-3.0

Two core dependencies ([Ultralytics](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics) and [BoxMOT](https://github.com/mikel-brostrom/boxmot))
are themselves licensed under AGPL-3.0, which means we also need to use this license.

## Non-commercial model restriction

Separately from the source license, the [InsightFace](https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface)
pretrained model weights that this application downloads are released **for non-commercial research purposes
only**.
